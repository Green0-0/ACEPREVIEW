**Programming and report**

- **Projects:** Discussion and collaboration are encouraged.
  - First project: Individual
  - Second project: Group
  - The two projects may be related or build upon each other
- **Research Question:** Clearly define a problem and goal. Refer to relevant previous research to explain the importance of the problem and position your research question objectively, rather than relying solely on personal motivation or interest.
- **Hypothesis & Experimentation:** Formulate a hypothesis and design an analysis or experiment to test it. Apply appropriate statistical methods to evaluate your hypothesis, including when using machine learning.
- **Data:** Focus on **human information processing**. Possible data include:
  - Text: corpus, conversational, or health-related data
  - Speech: spectrum, prosody, or voice characteristics
  - Physiological: EEG
  - Multimodal: integration of multiple data types
  - Other data related to human behavior and information processing
- **Analysis:** Simple statistical methods are acceptable when supported by a meaningful research question and hypothesis. Avoid projects based only on simple aggregation, descriptive statistics, or single numbers.
- **Datasets & Documentation:** Use open datasets (e.g., Kaggle). Include a simple README or GitHub repository explaining the code and its usage.
- **Creativity & Initiative:** Be creative and take initiative. If you are unsure about your project idea, please feel free to consult with me.
